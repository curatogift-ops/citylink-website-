City Link Community Guidelines

City Link aims to provide a respectful place to discover content and interact with other people.

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, intimidate or target other users.

Protect privacy

Do not share another person’s private information without permission.

Share responsibly

Do not post deceptive, unlawful or intentionally harmful material.

Respect content rights

Do not upload or distribute material unless you have the rights to do so.

Avoid spam and manipulation

Do not use bots, fake engagement or misleading activity to manipulate the platform.

Use reporting tools

Report abusive content, impersonation, suspected copyright infringement or security concerns in the app or through support.

Moderation

City Link may review reports and take action in line with the Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

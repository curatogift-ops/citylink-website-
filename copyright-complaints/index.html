Copyright and content complaints

City Link respects intellectual property rights. If you believe content available through City Link infringes your copyright or another right, send enough detail to identify the material.

Include this information

  • Your name and contact details.
  • The work or right involved.
  • The location or a clear description of the material.
  • Why you believe the use is unauthorised.
  • Evidence that you own the right or can act for the owner, where that applies.
  • A statement that the information is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Copyright contact: citylinkapp247@gmail.com

City Link reviews complete complaints and acts where the law and its role in providing the content require it. Content published by City Link and content played from a third party, such as YouTube, can follow different processes.

A response time is not promised until the operator sets one.

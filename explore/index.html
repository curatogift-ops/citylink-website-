Explore sports, videos and shows

These are the discovery categories in City Link. Watching the videos happens in the app. This page explains each category so the links are never empty.

Cricket and sports

Match listings, tournament pages and sports videos published by City Link.

Live coverage

Live matches open in the player when City Link has published a stream. If nothing is live, the app says so.

Events

Event collections group the videos that belong to one event, instead of listing every clip on its own.

Podcasts

Podcast shows are collections. Opening a show lists the episodes assigned to it.

Education

Learning collections can be named after a topic, person or programme. Each collection holds its own videos.

Films and series

Film and series collections keep related videos together.

News and daily updates

News can be a photograph or a video. Daily updates chosen by the City Link team appear on the app’s All tab.

About City Link

City Link is a digital platform built to bring sports, cricket, video content and community experiences together.

The platform helps people discover matches, follow available live coverage, explore published videos and shows, and use the community features provided in the app.

Making content easier to discover

The goal is a connected experience, so relevant sports and entertainment content does not live in disconnected places.

What City Link offers

  • Match and tournament information.
  • Live coverage when a stream is published.
  • Sports and other video content.
  • News and daily updates.
  • Shows, podcasts and content collections.
  • User profiles and the community features currently in the app.

Our approach

Clear discovery

Content should be easy to browse.

Connected experiences

Related videos, shows and match information stay organised.

Responsible operation

User data, community interactions and content rights are handled according to the published policies and applicable law. Those policies still need the operator’s confirmed details before public launch.

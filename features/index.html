Features

Explore what you can do with City Link

City Link brings sports and entertainment content into one mobile experience. The features below are the ones the app is built to provide.

01

Matches and tournaments

Browse available tournaments and the match information linked to them.

02

Live coverage

Open live content through the video player when a published stream is available. Viewing depends on the content source.

03

Videos and shows

Discover published videos in categories and collections, so related clips stay together.

04

News and daily updates

Browse published updates, photographs and videos. Image posts do not open a video player.

05

Personal profile

Set a display name, username and bio, and use the profile tools the app currently supports.

06

Community interaction

Follow accounts, like and share content, save items, and send a direct message where those features are enabled.

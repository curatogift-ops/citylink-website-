1. Introduction

City Link (“City Link,” “we,” “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, disclosed and protected when you use the City Link mobile application, website and related services.

Service operator: to be confirmed. Privacy contact: to be confirmed. Website: the official domain is to be confirmed.

2. Information we may collect

What is collected depends on the features you use.

Account information

Registration can include a full name, username, email address, an optional phone number, and the password handled by the authentication system. A profile photograph and biography can be stored when you supply them.

Profile and user content

Where those features are enabled, City Link may process profile details, posts, comments, follows, likes, saved items and direct messages.

Usage and technical information

This can include app activity, video viewing, device model, operating system, app version, diagnostic logs and identifiers used for sign-in, security or notifications. The exact analytics and crash tools must be confirmed by the operator.

Notifications

If push notifications are enabled, a device token may be processed to deliver them. You can control permission in your device settings.

Playback

Some videos play through YouTube. That provider processes information under its own privacy policy and terms: Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms.

Support

If you contact City Link, the name, email and message you send may be used to respond.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Sign people in and protect access.
  • Show profiles and content you submit.
  • Provide matches, videos, news, shows and other requested features.
  • Enable supported social features and messaging.
  • Send notifications you allow.
  • Keep the app working and investigate abuse or security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal duties.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where the law requires a basis, processing may rely on consent, providing the service you asked for, legitimate interests, or a legal obligation. Where consent is required, it is requested separately. Withdrawing consent can limit some features.

5. Sharing of information

City Link does not describe the sale of personal information as a business practice. Information may be shared with hosting and database providers, authentication and security providers, notification or diagnostic providers, video services, support tools, and advisers or authorities when the law requires it. The operator must name the actual providers before this policy is treated as final. Those providers have their own terms.

6. Public profiles and community content

Information you place on a public profile can be seen by other people. Do not post anything you want to keep private. Direct messages are intended for the people in that conversation. Reporting and blocking tools are available in the app.

7. Video services and external links

Third-party players and websites can collect information from your device when you use them. City Link does not control those practices.

8. Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No internet service can promise absolute security. Specific security claims are not made here.

9. Data retention

Information is kept for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including legal duties. Exact retention periods for accounts, messages, logs, support messages and backups must be confirmed before publication. No number of days is stated here.

10. Account deletion and data deletion

You can request deletion in the app under Profile, Settings, Delete Account, or on the account deletion page. A valid request leads to deletion of the account and associated personal data, except where the law requires something to be kept. Logging out is not deletion. City Link cannot promise deletion from a third-party system it does not control.

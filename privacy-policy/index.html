2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, City Link may process the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and authentication information

When you register, sign in or manage an account, we may process:

  • Full name or display name.
  • Username.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number, if requested or provided.
  • Authentication and account-security information handled by the authentication system.

The specific fields depend on the registration and sign-in methods available in the app. We do not ask users to send passwords or one-time passwords through the support contact form.

2.2 Profile and community information

Where profile or community features are available, we may process:

  • Profile photograph and biography.
  • Username and display name.
  • Posts, photographs, videos or other content submitted by users.
  • Follower and following information.
  • Likes, saved items and other supported interactions.
  • Messages sent through an in-app messaging feature, if enabled.

Information that you publish to a public profile or public-facing feature may be visible to other users. Review the available settings before publishing information.

2.3 Usage and activity information

We may process information about screens and features used, searches and content interactions, videos or other content opened or viewed, playback and viewing activity, and app performance.

2.4 Device and technical information

  • Device type and operating system.
  • App version.
  • Diagnostic and crash reports.
  • Security logs and technical identifiers.
  • Information required to deliver push notifications or maintain app functionality.

2.5 Photos, videos and device permissions

If a feature allows you to upload a profile picture, photograph, video or other media, City Link may process the selected file and the information needed to provide that feature. Camera, microphone, photo-library or other device permissions are requested only where a feature needs them.

2.6 Support and complaints

If you contact us, submit a support request, report content or make a privacy request, we may process your contact details and the information you provide. Please do not include passwords, OTPs, payment-card information or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

  • Deliver news, match information, tournaments, video content, podcasts, films, educational material and event updates.
  • Enable content discovery, playback and saved-content features.

  • Deliver notifications where enabled and permitted.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability and performance.
  • Protect accounts, prevent spam and detect misuse or security incidents.

Where consent is required by law, we will obtain it through an appropriate mechanism.

11. Account and personal data deletion

If you have a City Link account, you may request deletion through the in-app account settings, where available, or through the account deletion page. That page lets you start a deletion request without reinstalling the app.

We will verify requests where reasonably necessary to protect accounts from unauthorised deletion. Once a valid request is accepted, we will delete the account and associated personal data in accordance with the applicable process and legal requirements. If certain information must be retained for a lawful purpose, such as compliance, security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution, that exception is limited to what the law allows. Logging out, suspension or freezing an account is not account deletion.

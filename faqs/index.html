FAQs

Questions about City Link

These answers describe the app as it works today.

What is City Link?

City Link is a mobile app for cricket matches, tournaments, videos, news, shows and community features.

Where can I download the app?

The public Google Play listing is not live yet. See the download page.

How do I create an account?

Sign up with your full name, a unique username, email address and password. A mobile number is optional.

How can I find tournaments and matches?

Open the app home or live section. Tournaments have their own pages and stay separate from show collections.

Why is a video unavailable?

The video may be unpublished, removed, or missing a playable stream. The app shows that the content is unavailable instead of opening an empty player.

How do I update my profile?

Open My Profile, then Edit Profile, to change your display name, username and bio. Usernames must stay unique.

How do I contact support?

Use the contact page. Do not send your password.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the report and block tools in the app, or send a copyright or content complaint from the copyright page.

How can I request account deletion?

Use Delete Account in the app settings, or the account deletion page.

Where are the Privacy Policy and Terms?

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Both are drafts until the operator confirms the legal details.
